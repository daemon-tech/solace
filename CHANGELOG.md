# Changelog – Solace Shaders

Iris shaderpack for Minecraft 26.3 (tested with Iris 1.11.7, Sodium 0.9.2). All Rights Reserved.
All builds tested in the real client with screenshots.

## 0.35.1

### Changes
- **Dusk highlight roll-off**: at sunrise and sunset bright snow and cherry blossoms in the low warm sun roll off softly instead of clipping into a pale sheet, so their colour and texture stay (Dusk Highlight Roll-off setting). The rolled-off tones are gently calmed, so dusk blossoms glow a soft rose instead of a loud coral.
- **Wet sheen**: the cool wet sheen on crowns and grass tops in rain is a step stronger, so rainy meadows and forests read clearly.

## 0.35.0

A mood update: deep blue-teal nights and rain with warm windows, moonlit and wet crowns that keep their shape, luminous sunlit slopes, calm slate cliffs and sand paths, and richer teal forests.

### Changes
- **Far canopies**: distant dark forest crowns keep a little more of their deep green-teal under the blue distance haze.
- **Night teal**: at night land leaves the violet-grey navy for a deep, saturated blue-teal, while torch pools and cherry crowns keep their colour (Night Teal setting).
- **Rain teal**: in rain land leaves the violet-grey slate for a calm, deep blue-teal, while warm windows stay warm (Rain Teal setting).
- **Bright highlights**: by day the brightest lit land rolls up into a clean, luminous cyan-white, so sunlit slopes sparkle (Bright Highlights setting).
- **Forest teal**: by day tree crowns lose their grey red share and read as a rich, deep teal-green (Forest Teal setting).
- **Calm sand paths**: by day loud orange-yellow paths, sand and planks soften into a calm warm sand, so villages sit in the cool land (Calm Sand Paths setting).
- **Grass highlights balance**: bright flat grass keeps a fresh green instead of turning pale mint-white where meadow air and bright highlights overlap.
- **Moonlit crowns**: at night upward-facing tree crowns and grass tops catch a soft cool silver lift, so night forests keep their shape instead of one flat mass (Moonlit Crowns setting).
- **Cherry blossom colour**: by day sunlit cherry crowns keep their pastel pink instead of blowing out to white under the bright highlights.
- **Slate cliffs**: by day shaded rock settles from a saturated periwinkle wall into a calm blue-grey slate (Slate Cliffs setting).
- **Wet sheen**: in rain upward-facing tree crowns and grass tops catch a soft cool wet sheen, so rainy forests keep their shape in the dark blue air (Wet Sheen setting).

## 0.34.9

A calm depth update: deep, dark forests against light airy meadows, painted blue-teal shade, vivid flowers and warm golden grass at dusk.

### Changes
- **Meadow air**: by day bright and mid-bright open grass settles from saturated pure green into a lighter, airy blue-green (Meadow Air setting).
- **Forest depth**: by day near and mid-range tree crowns settle a calm step darker, while blossoms and distant haze stay untouched (Forest Depth and Deep Forest Tone settings).
- **Valley shade**: by day the darker shade on open land loses its vivid green and settles into a calm blue-teal (Valley Shade setting).
- **Deep canopy midtones**: by day the mid tones of tree crowns settle a step darker and richer, so forests read as deep painted green while sunlit highlights stay bright (Deep Canopy Midtones setting).
- **Flower pop**: flower petals keep vivid, saturated colour under the cool daylight, so meadows sparkle with bright dots of yellow, red and blue (Flower Pop setting).
- **Blue shade**: by day the deepest shade in tree crowns leans from vivid green-black into a calm, painted blue-teal (Blue Shade setting).
- **Shade lift**: by day the very darkest shade on land lifts a gentle step toward a luminous blue-teal instead of near black (Shade Lift setting).
- **Dusk grass gold**: at sunrise and sunset grass blades and grass tops turn a warm golden olive instead of fresh lime, so meadows and villages glow warm at dusk (Dusk Grass Gold setting).

## 0.34.8

A painterly light update: crisp leaves, luminous backlit crowns, cool shade with warm golden-hour light, calm slate cliffs, soft white snow, pastel cherry blossoms, travelling gust streaks on grass, darker forest floors and warm amber glow islands at night and in caves.

### Changes
- **Leaf texture pop**: by day leaves keep crisp texture contrast, with dark gaps and bright speckles, so tree crowns read as rich painted foliage instead of a flat green mass (Leaf Texture Pop setting).
- **Cool earth**: by day exposed dirt and paths lose their red-pink cast and settle into a soft olive umber that sits with the blue-green land, without turning grey (Cool Earth setting).
- **Cyan highlights**: by day the brightest sunlit grass and leaf tops roll off into a fresh cyan-white instead of yellow-lime, so lit hills and crowns sit in the cool daylight (Cyan Highlights setting).
- **Golden hour shade**: at sunrise and sunset faces turned away from the sun sink into a cool blue-teal, so warm lit sides glow against cool shade (Golden Hour Shade setting).
- **Golden hour light**: at sunrise and sunset sun-facing roofs, walls, rock and paths catch a warm peach light against the cool shade (Golden Hour Light setting).
- **Shadow pools**: by day sun-facing ground and crowns caught in a tree or cliff shadow sink into a deep blue-teal, so cast shadows read crisply in forests (Shadow Pools setting).
- **Gust silver**: travelling streaks of pale silver-mint sheen sweep across open grass with the wind, so gusts read across whole fields, also in calm spells (Gust Silver setting).
- **Torch-lit warmth**: at night surfaces inside a torch pool take a richer, saturated amber, so lit walls and paths glow against the navy, also in rain (Torch-Lit Warmth setting).
- **Soft snow**: by day sunlit snow and snowy slopes read as a soft cool white instead of a saturated blue sheet, also next to cherry groves and in ice fields (Soft Snow setting).
- **Cherry pink**: by day cherry blossoms keep a soft pastel pink under the cool daylight instead of turning white-lilac; nights keep their calm tone (Cherry Pink setting).
- **Forest floor shade**: by day the ground under a dense canopy sinks into a deep, cool teal shade, so forests read darker and calmer inside (Forest Floor Shade setting).
- **Calm stone**: grey rock and cliffs keep a cool slate grey under the blue daylight instead of turning a saturated blue wall; far mountains keep their airy blue (Calm Stone setting).
- **Sunlit translucency**: tree crowns seen against the sun glow through in a fresh, luminous light green, so backlit forests read light-filled (Sunlit Translucency setting).
- **Cave glow islands**: in caves torch and lava light forms warm, saturated amber islands against the dark, also by day (Cave Glow Islands setting).

## 0.34.2

A big look update: a clear, cool painted world with soft smooth shading, big soft clouds, a glowing sun, deep navy rain and nights, calm sandstorms, pale blue distance and valley mist, brighter turquoise water and warm orange torch light. Outlines are now optional and off by default.

### Changes
- **Rainbow after rain**: a fainter, calmer pastel arc.
- **Rain**: drops read as soft, slightly translucent pale-blue streaks; snow is unchanged.
- **Jungle sky**: the bright mint horizon is now a calm azure-cyan.
- **Nether and End**: overworld morning and valley mist no longer wash a pale veil over lava lakes and the void.
- **Biome skies**: jungles get a deep azure sky over a humid cyan horizon instead of green-teal; swamps a calm misty teal-grey instead of olive.
- **Rain by day**: a calm, lighter band of light sits low on the horizon under the overcast.
- **Night rain**: a few calm, dim blue moonlit beams now fall through the overcast too, like the daytime rain beams.
- **Far valley mist**: from high ground, distant valley floors lie under soft pale-blue mist sheets, so hills stack into calm layers (new Far Valley Mist options).
- **Sandstorm**: clouds fade away behind the dust, so the sky is one calm ochre haze instead of showing blue cloud patches.
- **Sandstorm**: the ochre dust wall closes in a little sooner and denser, like a real dust storm.
- **Night sky**: stars are drawn behind the painted clouds and the block cloud layers, so far fewer stars shine through clouds.
- **Sunrise**: sky and clouds glow a soft peach-gold instead of pink, with a calmer rim light on clouds near the sun.
- **Thunderstorms**: the overcast sinks a calm step darker and cooler than plain rain, so storms read heavier.
- **Snowfall**: the view below the horizon keeps the soft white-grey of the snow overcast, so no dark band sits under the pale sky.
- **Snowfall**: the spotlight beams of rain clouds fade out in snowfall and cold lands, so no bright vertical streaks stand over snowy hills.
- **Rain**: the sky, the block cloud layers and the gaps between clouds settle into one calm slate overcast by day (navy at night) instead of bright blue patches.
- **Soft sky-blue horizon**: on clear days the sky near the horizon rolls off softly into a calm sky-blue instead of clipping to a glaring mint-cyan; land and the sunrise/sunset colours are unchanged (Soft Horizon setting).
- **Peach-gold sunrise glow**: at sunrise and sunset the bloom stays a softer peach-gold glow instead of washing the horizon next to the sun to white; midday bloom and sun glare are unchanged.
- **Soft snowfall by day**: in snowfall, snowy ground and cold lands stay a soft cool white-grey instead of turning bright blue; rain elsewhere and the navy night are unchanged.
- **Rain clouds**: in rain the painted clouds turn a calm slate blue by day and a dark navy at night instead of staying bright white, a little darker still in thunderstorms and a soft cool grey-white during snowfall.
- **Spotlight beams in rain**: the light beams through rain clouds are narrower, brighter and gently slanted, so they read as calm spotlight shafts falling from gaps in the overcast.
- **Readable snowy nights**: in snowy lands at night and during snowfall the darkest tones lift gently, so snowy hills stay readable inside the calm navy mood (Snowy Night Lift setting).
- **More open sky between clouds**: the painted clouds leave a little more clear blue between the puffs, so each cumulus reads on its own.
- **Big soft painted clouds**: Soft Clouds are now on by default: large, softly shaded cumulus with pale tops and light-blue undersides instead of flat blocky slabs; they turn peach at sunset and settle into calm dark shapes at night (block clouds stay available in the Clouds menu).
- **One clean sun glow**: the older Sun Halo is off by default, since Sun Glare already draws the soft glow around the sun; the sun no longer gets a doubled wide haze (Sun Halo stays in the menu).
- **Whiter valley mist**: the mist lying in valleys is a softer near-white, so it stands out from the blue distance haze.
- **Gold and violet sunsets**: evening skies glow a warm gold at the horizon and rise through soft peach into a gentle pink-violet overhead, instead of deep orange all the way up.
- **Clouds keep their colour**: clouds (block clouds and painted clouds) are kept out of bloom, sun glare and the colour grades, so they no longer blow out to white, especially at dusk.
- **Ochre sandstorms**: deserts, badlands and savannas fade out of the navy rain mood, so sandstorms keep their warm ochre dust instead of turning navy-grey.
- **Calm sandstorms**: bad weather over deserts now raises a dense, calm ochre dust wall that swallows the distance (Sandstorm is on by default again), with only faint drifting streaks instead of busy lines.
- **Desert sky**: over deserts and other hot, dry lands the day sky turns a soft violet overhead and settles into a warm peach toward the horizon.
- **Valley mist you can see**: low land between hills now sits inside a soft pale veil that grows with distance, so valley floors read misty from hilltops and from inside the valley; the mist is smooth, without visible steps.
- **Starry nights**: clear nights show many more stars: a fine, still layer of small faint stars fills the sky between the bright soft ones, and fades at dawn, dusk and in rain.
- **Orange torch light**: torches, lanterns and fire cast a saturated orange glow, and their light pools stay warm orange inside the navy night and rain (Orange Torch Light setting).
- **Cherry blossoms at night**: pink crowns sink into the dark with the rest of the trees instead of glowing pale lavender.
- **Blue rain air**: rain fills the distance with a thick, calm deep-blue veil (Rain Fog is on by default again), lighter at night so the land stays readable, and by day a few broad soft pale light beams fall through gaps in the rain clouds (Rain Light Beams setting); the beams are broad and bright enough to read clearly while staying calm.
- **Cyan distance and valley mist**: far land fades into a paler, clearer cyan instead of grey-blue, the low valley mist is a little denser and lies higher, and the view past the world edge from high spots now settles into a soft blue-teal that matches the cyan horizon instead of a dark grey band.
- **Azure day sky**: the clear day sky is a deeper azure overhead that flows into a clear cyan near the horizon, with much less white glare along the horizon line.
- **Pale blue air**: distant land now softly fades into a pale cool blue with distance, and a calm low mist lies in valleys and over rivers (Aerial Perspective, Distance Blue and Valley Fog are on by default again).
- **Underwater light beams**: under water, soft slanted beams of light fall from the surface along the sun direction and sway slowly (Underwater Rays setting).
- **Smooth shading**: light now falls off in one soft, smooth gradient instead of visible light bands; contrast comes from colour, with neutral lit faces and deep blue-teal shade (Smooth Shading setting, 0 brings back stepped bands).
- **Outlines off by default**: shapes render clean and painted without edge lines; outlines stay available in the Outline settings.
- **Sun glare**: a bright soft glow with gentle radial streaks around the sun, plus stronger default bloom and light rays (Sun Glare setting).
- **Navy rain and night**: in rain and at night the world sinks into a calm, deep navy blue, almost one hue; rain turns clearly deep blue instead of grey (Navy Mood and Navy Rain Weight settings).
- **Far shading**: with smooth shading, far hills keep deeper blue-teal shade sides, so light and shade still read at long range (Far Shading setting).
- **Cyan underwater depths**: underwater, the water glows a clear cyan toward the surface and settles into a calm dark blue toward the depths below, with somewhat brighter light shafts.
- **Rolling surf**: soft white foam lines form out in the shallows and slowly roll in toward the shore, in long calm stretches along the coast.
- **Bright turquoise water**: water is a brighter, clearer turquoise that deepens into clean sky-blue, and looking out across it the surface keeps much more of the mirrored sky.
- **Cool air look**: the world now sits in a clear, cool blue-cyan light; shade turns a deep blue-teal instead of grey, and leaves and grass take a deeper, cooler green (less lime, less warm cast). The new Cool Air setting controls the strength; 0 brings back the warmer classic look.
- **Savanna gold**: in savannas and other hot, dry lands the grass keeps a warm golden-olive tone instead of meadow green (Savanna Gold setting).
- **Frosted tree tops**: in snowy lands the tops of tree crowns wear a soft pale frost (Frosted Tree Tops setting).
- **Soft cherry blossoms**: cherry blossom crowns settle into one calm pastel pink instead of busy speckled texels, with a soft glow where the sun falls (Cherry Blossom Glow setting).
- **Canopy depth**: leaves deep inside and under a tree crown settle into a cooler, deeper green while the outer leaves stay bright, so trees read with soft painted volume (Canopy Depth setting).
- **Canopy sheen**: sunlit tree tops get a calm, broad painted highlight, so forests seen from above read with soft light on their crowns (Canopy Sheen setting).
- **Fresh morning air**: for the first hours after sunrise the land sits in a fresh, slightly cool blue air before the day warms up (Fresh Morning Air setting).
- **Pale garden mood**: pale gardens turn quiet, muted and grey-green instead of bright lime (Pale Garden Mood setting).
- **Underwater light window**: looking up from under water, a soft bright window of sky light now opens in the surface above you while the water around it settles into a calmer deep teal; at night it stays a faint cool blue glow.

## 0.34.1

A calm sky update: softer sunrises, moon and Milky Way, gentler horizons in every weather, a warmer Nether and calmer End, darker soaked paths in rain, and Voxy support so far LOD land and water match the near look.

### Changes
- **Jungle sky**: over jungles the horizon settles into a calm, warmer green-teal, and the bright horizon glow keeps that tint instead of turning white.
- **Soft snowfall sky**: in snowy lands the overcast sky during snowfall settles into a gentle cool white instead of a dull grey.
- **Gentle Milky Way**: on clear nights the Milky Way band now has a soft painted glow along its core, so it reads as one calm river of light; it still fades at dawn, dusk and in rain.
- **Voxy far look**: with Voxy, far LOD terrain now gets Solace's cel lighting and grade (meadow-green grass tops, same shadow tint) instead of flat unshaded blocks, and far LOD water takes the same deep blue as near water without walls at section borders. Distance effects (aerial perspective, haze, distance blue and mountain layers) and the soft fade into the sky follow the Voxy view distance, so the vanilla chunk edge no longer fades early and leaves a seam.
- **Calm view past the world edge**: from high spots, the sky below the horizon now leans into a soft, darker grey-green land tone instead of pale sky colour, so it no longer reads as a fog wall, also in rain and thunderstorms; the world edge still melts in without a seam.
- **Soft sunrise**: dawn is one calm smooth gradient from pale gold near the sun through soft rose to a gentle blue above, less candy-pink than before.
- **Calm End sky**: the End sky is one smooth violet-dusk gradient from a soft rose-violet horizon to deep indigo, with a faint soft nebula glow instead of large blotchy bands.
- **Warm Nether glow**: with Nether fog off, the Nether gets a calm warm ambient tint, and rising embers plus the soft heat glow around lava are back. Both have their own settings (Nether Embers, Nether Warm Tint).
- **Soft moon**: the moon sits in one smooth silver glow with a soft pale disc instead of stepped rings.
- **Soft clear-day horizon**: on clear days the band at the horizon is a gentle pale blue-white gradient instead of a flat mint-turquoise strip, and the far edge of the world melts into it.
- **Soaked paths in rain**: open dirt paths, sand and stone now turn visibly darker with a soft grey sheen while it rains, a little more than lawns.
- **Soft evening clouds in hot lands**: savanna and desert clouds take a lighter peach tone at sunset, and the evening colour grade no longer pushes them to deep orange.

## 0.34.0

A big update: the world now moves with the wind, light sources glow and colour their surroundings, nights and golden hours read warmer and calmer, water got softer highlights and wet shores, and fog is gone so far land stays crisp.

### Changes
- **Clear skies, no fog**: distance, valley, cave, biome, weather, Nether and End fog and all distance haze are now off by default. Far land stays clear and keeps its colour, and the end of the render distance fades softly into the sky. Only short visibility limits remain under water, in lava and in powder snow (each fog option can still be turned back on in the menu).
- Water: calmer, softer sparkles that drift slowly.
- Water: wave tops facing the sun catch soft, warm painted highlights.
- Water: sand and soil at the water line now look softly wet (slightly darker, cooler strip).
- **Moon rim**: at night characters get a faint silver fringe from the moon (Moon Rim option).
- **Warm torch light at night outdoors**: torch and lantern pools in villages read as warm amber islands against the cool moonlight.
- **Stronger backlit leaves**: leaves seen toward the sun glow in a wider, warmer gold-green; grass tufts keep a gentle glow instead of neon specks.
- **Dripleaves and propagules in the wind**: big and small dripleaves sway like other plants, hanging mangrove propagules sway like hanging vines.
- **Bobbing lily pads**: lily pads bob gently and drift a little on the water with the breeze (Lily Pad Bob option).
- **More plants in the wind**: dry grass, dead bushes, sweet berry bushes, firefly bushes, saplings, eyeblossoms and cactus flowers now sway in the breeze too.
- **Morning dew**: in the first hours after sunrise, tiny dew drops on the grass near you twinkle in the sun (Morning Dew option).
- **Glowing redstone ore**: redstone ore that lights up when touched now glows red like other redstone lights.
- **Glowing furnaces**: lit furnaces, smokers and blast furnaces now glow warm from their fire opening and light the room.
- **Copper torches and lanterns**: copper torches and copper lanterns now glow with their green flame light, and hanging copper lanterns swing like the others.
- **Swaying spore blossoms**: spore blossoms under cave ceilings sway gently with the breeze like hanging vines.
- **Candle light**: lit candles and candle cakes count as warm light sources and get a soft warm glow around them (Candle Glow option). The tiny flames themselves are vanilla particles and do not flicker.
- **Cobwebs and leaf litter in the wind**: cobwebs billow softly and leaf litter stirs and slides a little when a gust passes (Web & Litter Wind option).
- **Glowing glow lichen**: glow lichen specks now glow softly and cast a faint green-teal light in caves.
- **Amethyst twinkle**: a few amethyst crystal facets catch a soft white twinkle now and then (Amethyst Twinkle option).
- **Glowing glow berries**: glow berries on cave vines now glow warm orange and light their surroundings, and the vines sway like other vines.
- **Swaying lanterns**: hanging lanterns and soul lanterns swing gently on their chain, a little more when a gust passes (Lantern Sway option).
- **Swaying reeds**: sugar cane and bamboo bend and ripple in the breeze as one stalk and lean downwind in a gust and their shadows sway with them (Reed Sway option).
- **Leaf glints**: during gusts a few sunlit leaf texels briefly catch the sun, a soft sparkle in the canopy (Leaf Glints option).
- **Swaying vines**: vines, cave vines, weeping vines and hanging roots move gently with the breeze, sliding along their wall without clipping into it.
- **Living flames**: torch and lantern light breathes softly, a little livelier when the wind picks up. Option "Flame Flicker" (0 keeps it steady).
- **Crops sway in the wind**: wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroots now bend with the breeze and the gusts like grass and flowers.
- **Torch flames keep their colour at night**: torch flames and lantern glass stay warm orange instead of fading to pale cream.
- **Cherry petals**: in cherry groves the drifting leaves become soft pink petals that sail on the breeze, a few more on each gust.
- **Drifting leaves**: loose leaves tumble down and sail away with the breeze near the ground, more of them when a gust passes. Option "Drifting Leaves" (0 turns them off).
- **Wooden roofs at late dusk**: roofs and paths no longer turn salmon pink in the last light; they keep a calm warm wood brown.
- **Soft savanna evenings**: in hot dry lands the evening sky settles into a calm soft peach with a gentler glow around the sun, instead of a deep orange wall.
- **Sea lanterns glow cool, not amber**: the ground around sea lanterns and end rods now takes their cool white light reliably instead of switching to a torch-like amber.
- **Leaves flip in the gusts**: when a gust passes over a forest, the leaves turn slightly paler and silvery, as if showing their undersides, and settle again behind the front.
- **Calmer redstone glow**: redstone lamps and torches tint the ground a soft red instead of a glaring one; torch light is unchanged.
- **Cyan soul light**: soul lanterns and soul torches cast a soft cyan glow on the ground around them.
- **Soul lanterns no longer glow amber**: coloured light now reaches the whole light pool, and the warm night grading only touches warm light, so soul fire gives a soft cool glow.
- **Whiter snow in the sun**: snow caps on trees and snowy ground further away read as soft white instead of icy blue, also when seen from a warmer biome.
- **Calmer blue hour on stone**: cobble and grey stone walls stay cool at blue hour without turning periwinkle.
- **No purple wood at night**: moonlit roofs and wooden walls read as a calm cool grey instead of lilac; torch-lit spots stay warm.
- **Calmer golden hour on dirt**: sunlit dirt and terracotta slopes at sunset stay warm without turning neon orange.
- **Pink cherry blossoms**: cherry leaves read as warm blossom pink again instead of cool lavender.
- **Richer sunlit treetops**: the brightest leaf faces in full sun keep a little green depth instead of washing out to a chalky pale lime.
- **Warm wood at sunrise**: wooden roofs, walls and paths stay warm wood brown in the morning light instead of turning salmon pink.
- **Moonlit gust waves**: at night the wind waves roll through the grass as a soft, cool silver shimmer.
- **Golden gust waves at sunrise and sunset**: with a low sun the wind waves in the grass glow soft gold instead of silver.
- **Gust waves through the grass**: each wind gust now rolls across meadows as a clear, soft silvery band, moving together with the swaying grass and the wind lines.
- **No more lavender stone at dawn**: grey stone and cobble walls in the open read as a soft warm grey in the dawn and dusk light instead of lilac.
- **Calmer sunny grass**: bright neon greens in full sun settle into a softer, warmer meadow green.
- **Warm torch-lit walls at night**: pale walls inside a torch pool glow warm cream instead of pink-lavender.
- **Torch light islands in caves**: underground and under roofs, torch light reads as a calm amber island, warm even on grey stone, glowing softly near the flame and fading gently; already warm or bright surfaces like wood stay calm.
- **Leaves in the rain**: tree crowns sit a calm step deeper when it rains instead of glowing pale lime.
- **Golden-hour sea**: in the evening light open water settles into a calm blue-teal with a hint of rose that matches the sky, instead of a pale minty green.
- **Golden sunrise**: the sunrise cast on the land is a soft peach-gold instead of a pink-magenta veil.
- **Softer waterfalls**: the foam tumbling down waterfalls has soft, cloud-like edges instead of hard-cut blue and white patches.
- **Gentle moonlight on water**: the moon path is a calm silver sheen with soft glints instead of a hard white disc and scattered flakes.
- **Calmer blue hour**: pale walls, quartz, stone and cobble no longer glow lavender-blue after sunset; they settle into a calm muted tone.
- **Night smoke**: campfire smoke and other particles no longer glow bright white at night; over a fire they take a soft dim ember tone.
- **Soft mirrored shores**: trees and hills reflected in calm water are softly smeared and sway gently with the waves instead of a sharp pixel copy.
- **Calmer far canopies**: from about 12 blocks away, leaf masses melt their bright/dark texel speckle into one soft painted shape; edges to sky and trunks stay crisp.
- **Quieter wind over trees**: the drifting wind lines only brush faintly over leaf blocks (tree crowns, also seen from above) and keep their full strength over meadows and grass.
- **Calmer underwater light**: under water the colour is a soft teal, the water brightens gently toward the sunlit surface, and light shafts fall in soft beams instead of hard patches.
- **Dappled sunlight**: the sun spots under leaf canopies are larger, softer and more frequent, so tree shadows read as dappled light instead of flat dark patches.
- **Smooth shore-to-deep water**: the colour now flows gently from bright shallows into deep blue instead of jumping in hard-edged steps.
- **Softer low-sun glints**: near sunset the sun's path on the water is softer and warmer, with fewer hard sparkles and no hard-edged bar.
- **Meadow patches**: lawns get calm, large drifts of sunny yellow-green and deeper cool green instead of one flat tone; grass blades share the patch of the block below.
