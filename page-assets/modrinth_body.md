![About](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daemon-tech/solace/master/page-assets/header_description.png)

</div>

Solace is a hand-built stylized shader pack that paints Minecraft in warm, soft light. It favours mood over realism, with glowing meadows, bright clear water and slow, colourful skies.

<div align="center">

![Features](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daemon-tech/solace/master/page-assets/header_features.png)

</div>

- **Soft cel lighting** with cool, colourful shadows instead of black ones
- **Light rays and soft bloom** filtering through tree canopies
- **Sunrise palette** with pink and peach tones and morning mist
- **Biome-aware fog and colour** that shifts with every biome
- **Bright stylized water** with foam streaks on the wave crests and horizon haze
- **Moon glint** across the water at night
- **Waving foliage and subtle outlines** (both can be turned off)
- **Low / Medium / High profiles** and detailed settings

<div align="center">

![Installation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daemon-tech/solace/master/page-assets/header_installation.png)

</div>

1. Install Fabric, [Sodium](https://modrinth.com/mod/sodium) and [Iris](https://modrinth.com/mod/iris) for Minecraft 26.3.
2. Drop the Solace `.zip` into `.minecraft/shaderpacks/` (don't unzip).
3. Select **Solace Shaders** under *Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs*.
4. Pick a profile in *Shader Pack Settings*.

**Compatibility:** Minecraft 26.3 · Iris 1.11.7 + Sodium ✅ · Bobby ✅ recommended · Distant Horizons ⚠️ not recommended yet · OptiFine not tested · tested on one GPU system so far.

**Known issues:** water reflects the sky only; foam on narrow streams can look strong; all block light shares one warm tint.

<details><summary><b>My FPS is low. What helps?</b></summary>
Use the Low or Medium profile, turn off light rays or shadows, and use Bobby instead of a huge render distance.
</details>

<details><summary><b>Do I need Whimscape?</b></summary>
No. Solace works with vanilla textures and any pack; Whimscape just matches the style.
</details>

<details><summary><b>Can I use it in videos or modpacks?</b></summary>
Videos and screenshots, including monetized ones: yes, a link back is appreciated. Modpacks: link to this page, don't include the file.
</details>
